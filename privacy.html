TouchToes

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TouchToes keeps your stretching data on your phone: your answers, your progress and your photos. There is no account. Only what's needed for your subscription goes to the app store and to RevenueCat, the service that checks it, and we only receive anything else if you email us. No ads, no tracking, and no analytics of how you use the app.

Who is responsible

TouchToes is made by Arunivio – Gabriel Varvodic, Reinwardtstr. 25, 42899 Remscheid, Germany (see the legal notice). For any question about privacy, write to support@toetouch.app.

What the app stores

The app saves the following in its own private storage on your phone, so it can plan your sessions and show your progress:

  • Your answers from the start of the app: your first name, age range, goal, activity level, how long you have felt tight, where you feel tight, what motivates you, your stretching experience, the time you like to stretch, and your reminder setting.
  • Your height, weight and goal weight. They are used only to suggest a goal weight and show an estimate.
  • Your challenge: your starting flexibility level, the day you are on, your streak, and the date and length of each finished session.
  • Progress photos you choose to take.
  • Two small settings: whether workout sounds are muted, and whether you have seen the app tour.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app connects to the internet only for your subscription (see Subscriptions).

Height, weight and health

Your height, weight and goal weight can count as health information. They never leave your phone.

Camera and photos

TouchToes asks for camera access only when you open the progress photo screen. Photos are saved inside the app, not in your photo gallery, and are never uploaded. The app doesn't use the microphone and can't see your other photos.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, TouchToes asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet. If you start a free trial and allow notifications, TouchToes also reminds you, the same way, the day before the trial ends.

Backups of your phone

If you have turned on backups for your phone (for example Google backup on Android or iCloud Backup on iPhone), they can include TouchToes' data, as they do for other apps. Those backups are kept in your Google or Apple account under their terms. We can't see them.

App stores

Apple and Google may show us download numbers and, if you have agreed to share them, crash reports. These don't include your name or anything you enter in TouchToes.

Subscriptions

TouchToes opens with a free trial or a subscription, which you buy through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes your payment under their own terms and privacy policies (Apple, Google). We don't receive your payment details.

To check and restore your subscription, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA), which processes the following on our behalf:

  • a random ID that the app creates for your installation (not your name, email address, or a device or advertising ID);
  • your purchases: the plan, price and currency, dates, the store's order and transaction numbers, and whether a trial or subscription is active;
  • technical details such as your store country, your language and currency settings, the phone's operating system and the app version;
  • your IP address, as with any connection to a server.

We use this to open TouchToes while your trial or subscription is active, to restore it on a new phone and to answer your questions about it. The legal basis is our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). RevenueCat also shows us statistics about subscriptions, such as how many trials and subscriptions started, renewed or were cancelled, which we use to run and plan TouchToes. This is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object to it (see Your rights). The random ID and your subscription status are also saved on your phone so the app can check your access; this is strictly necessary for the subscription you asked for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

RevenueCat stores this data in the USA. The transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in our data processing agreement with RevenueCat (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). We keep purchase records while your trial or subscription runs, and afterwards only as long as we need them to answer questions about it or the law requires us to.

Emails to us

If you write to support@toetouch.app, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you, and delete them once your question is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer (for example business and tax records). Please don't send health details we don't need to help you.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when your message concerns a contract with us, such as your subscription, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Our email is run by Zoho Corporation GmbH (Essen, Germany), which processes it only on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) and stores it in its data centres in the European Economic Area. Zoho group companies in India may access it when needed for technical support and debugging, and Zoho uses the sub-processors it lists at zoho.com/privacy/sub-processors.html. Under that agreement, Zoho must protect any transfer outside the European Economic Area with a safeguard the GDPR recognises (Art. 44–49 GDPR); you can ask us for details. After we delete an email, it can remain in Zoho's encrypted backups for up to three months.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete progress photos removes all photos and keeps your workouts.
  • Settings → Delete all app data removes everything, turns off your reminder and starts TouchToes over.
  • Uninstalling TouchToes also removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

TouchToes is only for adults aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children. If you answer that you're under 18, the app stops at that question. The answers given until then stay only on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Your rights

Because the app's data stays on your phone, we can't see, change or delete it; you can, in the app. For your subscription records, for emails you have sent us and for what you send us on TikTok or Instagram (see our profiles), you can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion, a restriction of their use, or a copy in a portable format, and you can object to our use of them (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Write to support@toetouch.app for this or any other question; for subscription records, include the order number from your Apple or Google receipt so we can find them. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.
